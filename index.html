Privacy Policy

How BillOne handles information when you use our business management app.

Effective date: August 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to BillOne ("BillOne", "we", "us", or "our"). BillOne is a business management application that helps businesses manage sales, purchases, invoices, inventory, products and services, customers, suppliers, expenses, payments and business reports.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, how it may be stored or shared, and the choices available to you when you use BillOne.

By using BillOne, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

2.1 Business and account information

Depending on the features you use, BillOne may collect information such as:

  • Business or shop name
  • Business address and contact details
  • GSTIN and other tax-related business details
  • Business logo or other information you choose to add
  • Account information, such as your name, email address or phone number, if an account is created

2.2 Transaction information

Information you enter into BillOne may include sales, purchases, invoices, invoice numbers, products, services, prices, quantities, discounts, taxes, payment status, expenses and other business records.

2.3 Customer and supplier information

You may choose to enter information about your customers and suppliers, such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, addresses, GSTINs and transaction or outstanding-balance information.

2.4 Device and technical information

We may receive limited technical information necessary to operate, secure and improve the app, such as app version, device type, operating system, crash information and diagnostic information. The exact information collected depends on the services and analytics or crash-reporting technologies enabled in the app.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate BillOne's features
  • Create and manage invoices and business records
  • Calculate and display sales, purchases, taxes, balances and reports
  • Manage products, services and inventory
  • Manage customers and suppliers
  • Maintain and improve app performance and reliability
  • Detect, prevent and address fraud, abuse, security issues and technical problems
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important service information
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations
Your business data belongs to your business. BillOne does not sell your business records, customer lists, invoices or inventory information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Depending on the BillOne version and features you use, your information may be stored locally on your device, on BillOne's servers, or through trusted service providers used to operate the app.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share information only when reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure or legally operate BillOne, including with:

  • Cloud hosting, database, storage and infrastructure providers
  • Analytics, crash-reporting or technical service providers, where enabled
  • Customer support and communication service providers
  • Professional advisers or authorities when required by law

Service providers are expected to process information only for the purposes for which they are engaged and subject to appropriate contractual or legal protections.

We may also disclose information if required to comply with applicable law, a valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety and security of BillOne, our users or others.

6. Sharing Invoices and Business Records

BillOne may allow you to export, download, print or share invoices and other business information. When you choose to share information using your device's sharing features, the information is sent to the person, service or application you select.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have an appropriate legal basis and permission to enter and share personal information belonging to your customers, employees, suppliers or other third parties.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain business records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations and enforce agreements.

If you delete your account or request deletion, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize information that we are not required to retain. Some information may remain for a limited period where required by law, for legitimate security purposes, or as part of backups.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of information, subject to legal exceptions
  • Request restriction of certain processing
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request a copy of certain information in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise an applicable right, contact us using the details in the "Contact Us" section below.

9. Children's Privacy

BillOne is intended for businesses and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is prohibited by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

10. Third-Party Services and Links

BillOne may use third-party services to support hosting, authentication, analytics, crash reporting, notifications or other functionality. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

If BillOne links to an external website or service, that service is not controlled by this Privacy Policy. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party service before providing information to it.

11. International Data Transfers

Depending on the infrastructure and service providers used by BillOne, information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where you live. Where required by applicable law, we will use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in BillOne, applicable law or our data practices. When we make changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or another appropriate method.
