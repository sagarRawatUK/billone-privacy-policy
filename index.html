1. Who is responsible

BillOne is an offline-first GST invoicing, billing and inventory app for small businesses. It is developed and published by Sagar Singh, an individual developer based in India, under the developer name “Myx”. BillOne is not published by a company.

In this policy, “I”, “me” and “my” mean Sagar Singh. I decide how the information described here is used and I am responsible for it. Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 I am the “data fiduciary”, and under the GDPR I am the “data controller”. I am also the grievance contact for BillOne.

2. Information stored on your device

Everything you enter is saved in a local database on your phone:

  • your business profile, including business name, address, GSTIN, PAN and bank details;
  • customers and suppliers you add, such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, addresses and GSTINs;
  • products, services, stock and stock movements;
  • invoices, payments, recurring subscriptions and reports.

This data does not leave your device unless you use Cloud Sync, export it or share it. Files you import (for example Excel or CSV product lists) are read only on the device.

3. Cloud Sync (optional)

If you create an account and use Cloud Sync, the records listed above are copied to a cloud database hosted by Supabase so you can back them up and use them on another device.

  • Your account is an email address and password. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are never visible to me.
  • Every synced record is tied to your account and protected by row-level security, so only you can read or change it.
  • Sync runs only when you tap Sync. Nothing is uploaded in the background.
  • You can sign out at any time, and your local data stays on the device.

4. Purchases (BillOne Pro)

Subscriptions and the one-time Pro purchase are sold through Google Play or the Apple App Store and managed by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an app user ID (anonymous, or your account ID once you sign in) and the store’s purchase receipt so Pro can be restored on your other devices. I never receive your card or bank details.

5. Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device’s advertising ID, IP address and information about your device and app use to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, BillOne asks for your consent first through Google’s consent form, and on iOS through Apple’s tracking prompt. Ads may be personalised or not, depending on your choice. BillOne Pro removes all ads, and the ad SDK makes no ad requests for Pro users.

6. Analytics (Meta App Events)

BillOne uses the Meta (Facebook) SDK to measure app installs and the results of ad campaigns. The SDK logs installs, app opens and session time automatically, and the app sends three events: business setup completed, an invoice issued, and a Pro purchase with the product and its price. Events never include your business details, customers, products or invoice contents. The SDK may also collect your device’s advertising ID and device information. On iOS, access to the advertising identifier follows your App Tracking Transparency choice. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

7. What I do not collect

  • Contacts, location, photos or messages.
  • Analytics or tracking other than the AdMob ads and Meta events described above.

I do not sell, rent or trade your data, and I do not use your invoices or customer lists for marketing.

8. Sharing invoices

When you export or share an invoice or report (PDF, Excel or CSV), it goes to the app or person you choose through your device’s share sheet. You are responsible for having permission to store and share your customers’ and suppliers’ details.

9. Service providers

  • Supabase: database and authentication, only if you use Cloud Sync.
  • RevenueCat: purchase management, only if you buy or restore Pro.
  • Meta: app event analytics and install measurement.
  • Google AdMob: advertising, free version only.
  • Google: Google Play: app distribution and payments.
  • Apple: App Store: app distribution and payments.

I may also disclose information if the law requires it, for example because of a valid court order.

10. How long data is kept

  • Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud data stays until you delete your account.
  • Analytics and ad data are kept by Meta and Google under their own retention policies.
  • Purchase records are kept by the app stores and RevenueCat as their policies and tax laws require.

11. Deleting your account

You can delete your BillOne account and its data at any time, in the app or without it.

In the app

  1. Open Cloud Sync.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Contact me from the email address on your account and ask me to delete it. I will confirm the account is yours, delete it within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • your account (email address and password);
  • every record synced to the cloud, permanently and straight away.

What is kept

  • The data on your phone, which stays until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. Without an account, this is the only copy, and uninstalling deletes it.
  • Copies in the hosting provider’s encrypted backups, until those backups expire a short time later.
  • Purchase records, which Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat keep as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Analytics and ad data held by Meta and Google, under their own retention policies.

12. Your rights and choices

You can view, edit, export (PDF, Excel, CSV) and delete your records inside the app.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to:

  • access the personal data I hold about you and get a copy in a portable format;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to (India).

To make a request, contact me. I will reply within 30 days and may need to confirm that the data is yours first. If you are not satisfied with my answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India or, in the EEA or UK, to your local data protection authority.

13. Security

Cloud data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by the hosting provider, and access is restricted to the signed-in account. Data on your phone is protected by your device’s own security, such as its screen lock and encryption. No system is completely secure, but if a breach affects your data I will notify you and the relevant authorities where the law requires.

14. Children

BillOne is not intended for anyone under 18 because it is a business tool. I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided information through BillOne, contact me and I will delete it.

15. Where your data is processed

I am based in India. The service providers named above may store or process data in other countries, including the United States and the European Union. Where data leaves the EEA or UK, the transfer relies on safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses in the provider’s terms.

16. Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I will update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes I will also let you know in BillOne before they take effect.

17. Contact

For questions about this policy, data requests or complaints, contact me by email. I built the app, so I will answer your message myself.
